3. 谁能看到你的数据

  • 所有登录用户：你的用户 ID、名字、国家、头像（为了让朋友能按 ID 加你）。
  • 你的好友：你的全部打卡、照片及其评论。只有互相确认的好友才能看到，你可以随时删除好友。
  • 私信：只有收发双方能看到。
  • 群聊：群名、成员名单和消息只有群成员能看到，新成员看不到入群之前的消息；你转发到群里的章，群成员能看到那几张照片，退群后就看不到了。群成员能看到你读到了哪条消息（「几人已读」）。
  • 聊天里转发的明信片：对话里的人（私信的对方、群成员）都能看到这张明信片下的评论；只有发帖人的好友可以评论。
  • 运营者：在管理员控制台里能看到汇总统计，以及用户列表（ID、名字、头像、注册时间、最近活跃时间，以及打卡、照片、好友、评论、消息的数量）；看不到私信、评论、明信片的文字和照片。

6. 保存期限

  • 账号数据保存到你注销账号为止。注销后立即删除，数据库备份中的副本最迟在 30 天内被覆盖。
  • 你删除的单条打卡会留下一个不含内容的删除标记，用来同步到你的其他设备；照片会立即删除。
  • 服务器日志由服务商保存，一般不超过 90 天。
  • 使用统计保存 400 天后自动删除；注销账号时随账号一起删除（只留下一条不关联任何人的「有人注销了账号」记录）。

7. 安全措施（GDPR 第 32 条）

  • 传输加密：所有连接只使用 HTTPS（TLS）。
  • 存储加密：数据库和照片存储使用 AES-256 磁盘加密；备份同样加密。
  • 密码只保存 bcrypt 哈希，任何人（包括我们）都看不到原始密码。
  • 数据库对每张表启用行级权限（RLS），每个请求都按「只能访问自己和好友授权的数据」校验；照片存放在私有存储桶中，只有你本人登录后、或你的好友通过短时有效的签名链接才能访问。
  • 注册需要邀请码；生产环境的密钥不会出现在网页代码中。
  • 你的打卡还会缓存在你自己设备的浏览器（IndexedDB）中，方便离线使用。请勿在公共设备上保持登录。

如果发生可能危害你权利的数据泄露，我们会在知悉后 72 小时内报告监管机构，并及时通知受影响的用户。

  • Group chats: the group name, member list and messages are visible only to members, and new members can’t see messages from before they joined. Members can see the photos of a stamp you share in the group until they leave. Members can also see how far you have read (“Read by 3”).

5. Maps and place search

6. Retention

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account. It is then deleted immediately; copies in database backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • When you delete a single check-in, an empty deletion marker is kept so the deletion syncs to your other devices. Its photos are deleted at once.
  • Server logs are kept by our providers, normally for no more than 90 days.
  • Usage statistics are deleted automatically after 400 days, and with your account when you delete it (leaving only an anonymous “an account was deleted” record).

7. Security (Art. 32 GDPR)

  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS (TLS) only.
  • Encryption at rest: the database, photo storage and backups use AES-256 disk encryption.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Nobody, including us, can see your password.
  • Row-level security on every table checks each request, so you can access only your own data and what friends have shared with you. Photos are in a private bucket, reachable only by you when signed in or by your friends through short-lived signed links.
  • Sign-up requires an invite code. Production secrets are never shipped in the web page.
  • Your check-ins are also cached in your own browser (IndexedDB) for offline use. Do not stay signed in on shared devices.

If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell affected users without undue delay.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability (Art. 15, 20): in Settings → Data, use “Download my data” for a JSON file of your account, friends, comments, messages and group chats, “Export backup” for all check-ins and photos, and “Download photos” to save photos by year and place.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): edit your profile and check-ins directly.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): use “Delete account” in Settings → Data. Your account, profile, check-ins, photos, friendships, comments, messages and group messages you sent are permanently deleted at once.
  • Restriction, objection (Art. 18, 21) and withdrawing consent (Art. 7): email us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.
  • Complaint (Art. 77): you can complain to the data protection authority of your EU member state.

9. Local storage

We use only strictly necessary browser storage: your sign-in session, interface language and theme, sync progress and offline check-in cache. None of it is used for tracking, so no cookie consent is needed.

10. Age

You must be at least 16 years old to sign up. If we learn that someone under 16 has signed up, we will delete the account.
